Pilot

Start with one workload

We work directly with a small number of AI startups. A pilot is free on one workload, runs in your infrastructure, and is reviewed against your own evals.

Email founders@qekto.com
Week 0

Scope

A short call about your product, providers, data constraints and the workload you most want to own. We agree on success criteria and on what counts as ground truth.

Day 1

Deploy and connect

Qekto runs in your cloud as one container with a volume. You add your provider, create a scoped key, and change your base_url. Traffic flows through unchanged from the first request.

Weeks 1–3

Measure and label

You see real volume, latency, tokens, estimated cost and errors per workload. Ground truth arrives through labels or outcomes you already track. The workload contract records task, schema, evaluator and training rights.

When ready

Graduate behind a gate

If a compatible trainer exists, we train, evaluate on a frozen holdout, and review the release card together. A model serves traffic only after it passes the gate, a shadow run and a canary.

A good fit if

  • You make thousands of similar model calls a day: routing, triage, extraction or tool steps.
  • You have, or can collect, ground truth for that task.
  • You can run one container in your environment.

Terms

  • Free on one workload during the pilot.
  • After the pilot, you choose between a share of measured savings and a monthly upkeep fee.
  • Trained weights, release cards and eval data are yours to export at any time.
