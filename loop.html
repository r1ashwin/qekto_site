Measure

See your gaps, the competition, and why AI won’t recommend you.

We ask ChatGPT and Claude the questions your buyers ask, then report what they said.

01 Mention rate by brand

Every brand you track, scored on the same questions. How often each one is named. 0% means agents never said its name.

02 The result

Your headline numbers. Named in answers is how often you come up at all. Share against rivals is your slice when more than one tracked brand shows up in the same answer.

03 Each agent, separately

ChatGPT and Claude don’t always agree. You see each one’s rate on the same questions, so you know where you are weak.

04 Why they pick you, and where they push back

The reasons agents give, taken from their answers. What to lean into, and what is costing you.

05 Sources agents cited

The sites agents linked to when they answered. These pages shape the answer, and they are where you want to show up.

06 Questions tested, and who else came up

The exact questions we asked, so every number can be checked. Plus brands agents named that you don’t track yet.

Run it again and the report shows what changed since the last check. You can also add a market, compare shopper types, or check another brand.
