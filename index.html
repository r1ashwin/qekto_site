The loop

From rented API calls to owned, evaluated models

AI products send the same narrow task to frontier APIs thousands of times a day. Qekto runs one loop over that traffic, and every step leaves an audit trail.

01 · Capture

Forward and record

Requests are forwarded byte-for-byte, including streaming, tools, structured outputs, and image, audio and file parts. Qekto records latency, tokens, cost estimates, errors and typed content metadata under your storage policy.

02 · Understand

Workload contracts

Calls are grouped into workloads. Each one gets a versioned contract: task family, modalities, output schema, evaluator, SLOs, privacy, and the training rights you have declared.

03 · Graduate

Capability-matched training

Training starts only when there is a legally usable source of ground truth and a trainer, evaluator and runtime that can handle the workload. If something is missing, Qekto names it.

04 · Gate

Frozen holdout evals

Each candidate is scored against a deterministic, frozen holdout and compared with your current model, slice by slice and case by case. Versions that fail the gate never receive traffic.

05 · Roll out

Shadow, canary, rollback

A passing version first runs in shadow on live traffic, then serves a canary percentage. It is promoted or rolled back automatically from observed fallback rates, and invalid outputs fall back to your provider.

06 · Improve

Retrain as data grows

New labels and new traffic feed the next version under the same gate. You can run graduation by hand or let Qekto's policy train and canary automatically.

Never worse, nothing hidden

What Qekto refuses to do

These guarantees come from how the software is built, not from a policy page.

  • Train on closed-provider outputs. Captured upstream answers become training data only if you declare the right to use them on both the workload and the upstream.
  • Pretend it can graduate a workload. If no compatible trainer, evaluator or runtime exists, the workload stays on your provider and the missing capability is shown.
  • Send traffic to a version that failed its evaluation gate.
  • Store base64 media in its database. Raw images, audio and files are kept only if you opt in, and in object storage.
  • Show a number it did not measure. Every chart comes from your own traffic, and cost figures are labeled as estimates from the prices you set.
  • Mix customers. Organizations and projects are isolated, and Qekto runs in your infrastructure.
