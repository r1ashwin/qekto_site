Security and data handling

Your traffic stays yours

Qekto is a data processor. Your traffic is used only to build and run your own models. It is never mixed across customers and never used for shared models.

Self-hosted

Qekto runs in your infrastructure, as a single container with a persistent volume. Upstream provider calls go directly from your environment to your provider.

Isolation

Organizations, projects and environments are separate. Every query is scoped to the caller's project, and projects in other organizations do not appear to exist.

Keys and credentials

Project API keys are shown once and stored as salted hashes. Scoped keys can be rotated and revoked, effective on the next request. Provider credentials are encrypted at rest, never returned by any API, and replaced only after the new key passes a live test.

Dashboard access

Owner, admin and member roles. Sessions use HttpOnly cookies with CSRF protection and a strict content security policy, with no inline scripts.

Storage policy

Per project: pause capture, store no content, PII-redacted content (the default) or full text, opt-in raw media in object storage, and retention in days. Base64 media is never written to the database.

Export and deletion

Export a project's configuration, traffic records, labels, model artifacts, jobs and audit log as a zip, which excludes credentials and key hashes. Delete a project or organization and its data, media and artifacts are removed.

Audit trail

Every contract change, training run, gate decision, shadow, canary, promotion, rollback, policy change and key or credential operation is recorded with actor and time.

Safe failure

Unsupported endpoints are rejected before uploads are read. Qekto never fetches URLs found in payloads. If analytics fail, proxy traffic still flows.

Training rights

Only data you have the right to use

Many model providers' terms prohibit training competing models on their outputs. Qekto enforces this in the data path. Before any example reaches a trainer, its source must be declared in the workload contract:

  • Customer labels: ground truth you provide.
  • Oracle labels: answers from your own system of record.
  • Open teachers: outputs of license-compatible open-weight models.
  • Upstream outputs: accepted only if you also flag that upstream as training-allowed. Both declarations are audited.

Provider answers can still serve as an evaluation baseline and shadow reference. They become training data only after both declarations above.

Scope note. PII redaction is pattern-based. It reduces stored PII but is not a compliance certification. Tell us your requirements (for example HIPAA or GDPR data residency) and we will say plainly what v1 does and does not cover.
